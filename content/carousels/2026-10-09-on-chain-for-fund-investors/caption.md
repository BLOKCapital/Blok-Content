You can see your fund's statement. But can you see your fund?

If you invest in mutual funds or ETFs, your money moves through a fund house, a custodian, a registrar and a broker. They all do real work. They also add time, cost, and layers you never see.

On-chain investing runs the same idea on different rails:
→ Holdings visible any time, not just monthly or quarterly
→ Settles in minutes, not days
→ Open 24/7
→ Every move recorded on a public ledger you can check yourself

It's newer, and it comes with its own risks. That's why we start with learning.
