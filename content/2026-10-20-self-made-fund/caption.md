Billionaire's son: "I started my own fund" 😌

The joke works because a fund has always needed a rich dad. Many funds that are managed around you ask for six figures just to get in: in India a PMS starts at ₹50 lakh and an AIF at ₹1 crore, before the setup fees and the bank's introduction fee.

Funny thing is, the first fund was built for the opposite crowd. Amsterdam, 1774: a broker pooled savings so "investors of limited means" could spread their money around too.

Today you don't need the dad. An on-chain portfolio is your own fund, in an account only you control, starting from $10. It's newer and prices can swing, so start small and learn first.

What would you put in your $10 fund? 👇
