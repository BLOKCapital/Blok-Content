## LinkedIn
Wall Street's $114 trillion vault is moving on-chain, and it starts this month.

DTC, part of DTCC, holds custody of more than $114 trillion in US securities. Its tokenization service goes fully live in October 2026, after limited production trades in July. More than 50 firms helped design it.

The first assets covered by the SEC's December 2025 no-action letter are the core of most portfolios:
→ Russell 1000 stocks
→ ETFs tracking major indexes
→ US Treasury bills, notes and bonds

Each tokenized version carries the same entitlements and ownership rights as the original.

Why this matters beyond the back office: for decades, investors have learned what they own from a statement, after the fact. On a shared ledger, the record exists as it happens and can be verified.

It's early. Rules are still forming, and on-chain markets carry real risks. But when the plumbing under index funds changes, every investor should understand it.
