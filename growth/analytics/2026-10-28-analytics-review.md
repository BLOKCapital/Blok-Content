## Headline

1. **Instagram followers went 66 → 440 in eight days, but almost all of it is the boost, and it's the wrong crowd.**
   Paid delivery was 96% of reach this week (28,342 of 29,455). 90% of followers Instagram can place are in Spain
   (353 of 391) and half are 55+. They follow, but they don't comment or share. The 24 Oct fix (custom audience) was
   not applied, and the boost is still running (2,963 paid reach yesterday).
2. **Our Reels got better to watch, but organic reach halved.** Median avg watch went 5.3s → 7.5s and median skip
   74% → 64% after cutting length. But organic reach per Reel fell from ~160 to ~100, and comments and shares
   nearly vanished (4 comments, 3 shares across 11 Reels). The "send this to…" endings aren't working.
3. **One theme wins on both platforms: "your money keeps office hours"** (Wall Street on Saturdays until 1952,
   "your money has a 9-to-5", "AI took my emails… it didn't"). Best skip rates on IG (48 to 52%) and the top organic
   X post of the week (705 views). It's exactly the history-fact-meets-today pattern.
4. **X is flat to falling.** Followers 5,772 → 5,726 (−46). 21 posts, all between 190 and 705 views (median ~450).
   Nothing broke 1,000, the stance posts didn't repeat last week's breakout, and replies still go to the same small ring.
5. **Carousels are dead weight.** 3 this week reached 33, 29 and 7 accounts.
