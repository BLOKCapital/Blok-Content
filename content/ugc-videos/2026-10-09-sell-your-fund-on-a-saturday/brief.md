## Shot list
1. **A-roll:** one continuous selfie take on a couch, natural window light, phone at eye level, vertical 9:16, 4K/1080p 30fps.
2. **Cutaways (optional, 1–2s each):** thumb tapping a generic "Sell" button on a blurred screen; a wall clock or a phone lock screen showing "Saturday"; a coffee mug.
3. Do **not** show any real broker or fund app logos, and nothing that looks like a trading chart or coins.
4. Keep it imperfect: one jump-cut per beat is fine, and so is a small smile or stumble.

## On-screen style
Inter Bold, white text with a dark outline, or black text on a linen (#F6F3E8) box. Keep text within the
central safe zone (avoid top 220px and bottom 380px). Use one lime (#7ED116) accent max, on "open 24/7".
