In 1888, a novelist predicted the credit card. 62 years early. 👇

Edward Bellamy's book Looking Backward imagined Boston in the year 2000. No cash, no coins. Just a paper card, and the clerk punches out the value of each purchase.

He even called it a credit card. The first real multipurpose card, Diners Club, arrived in 1950.

He got paying right. What he missed: paying got easy for everyone, but owning a fund didn't. Many private funds still ask for $100,000 or more just to get in.
