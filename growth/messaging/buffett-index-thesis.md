## The thesis in one breath
Buffett's advice for most people has always been boring: don't pick stocks, don't time the market, don't pay expensive
managers; own a very low-cost broad index and keep buying. That advice works because most people want diversified,
low-friction, set-and-forget exposure, not complexity. **BLOK Capital gives that same simplicity on-chain, without giving
up custody or control.**

## The argument, step by step
1. **Buffett's rule for the many.** For decades Buffett has told ordinary investors not to stock-pick, time the market
   or hire costly managers, and to put the bulk of their equity money in a very low-cost S&P 500 index fund.
   For his wife's trust he instructed 90% in a low-cost S&P 500 index fund and 10% in short-term government bonds.
2. **The know-nothing investor wins.** "By periodically investing in an index fund, the know-nothing investor can actually
   out-perform most investment professionals." (Berkshire Hathaway letter, 1993). After fees, most pros trail the index.
3. **Why it held up:** people want diversified, low-friction, set-and-forget exposure.
4. **The gap:** traditional index products still route people through custodians, minimums, market hours and intermediaries.
5. **What BLOK enables:** on-chain indices and wealth tools: auto-rebalancing Index Gardens, market-cap or rules-based
   Indices, and self-managed Gardens. Buffett-style simplicity, **without handing over the keys**.
6. **Self-custody + verifiable rules:** users stay in their own wallet; nothing moves unless the smart contract allows it;
   the rules are transparent and checkable on-chain.
7. **Why it matters for tokenized real-world assets (RWAs):** diversified exposure, automatic rebalancing, collateral
   utility, yield layering and easy composability with the rest of DeFi, without repeated KYC friction or middlemen
   holding the keys. This gives people natural reasons to hold and use tokenized RWAs.
8. **Result: broader distribution.** The 99% Buffett was talking to finally get an on-chain equivalent they can use.
   That drives secondary activity, liquidity, and institutional confidence that these markets are real.
