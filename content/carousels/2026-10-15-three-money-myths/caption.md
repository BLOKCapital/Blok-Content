## Instagram
3 money myths fund investors still believe 🌱

1️⃣ "I own 3 funds, so I'm diversified." Three fund names can still hold the same giant companies at the top. Put each fund's top 10 holdings side by side and count the repeats.

2️⃣ "A 1% fee is tiny." On $10,000 growing at 7% a year for 30 years, a 1% yearly fee costs about $18,700. Illustrative maths, not a forecast.

3️⃣ "My statement shows what I own." It shows your units. The fund's actual holdings are disclosed periodically and held by a custodian for you. On-chain portfolios flip that: you hold the assets and can check them any time. The catch: it's newer tech, and prices still swing.
