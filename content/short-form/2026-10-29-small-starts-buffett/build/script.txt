He sold his first stock too early. Guess who.
1942. An 11-year-old buys three shares for $114.
They drop to $27. He holds.
Back at $40, he sells.
Then they climb past $200.
That kid was Warren Buffett.
Charlie Munger: "The big money is not in the buying and the selling, but in the waiting."
John Bogle: "Don't look for the needle in the haystack. Just buy the haystack!"
Buffett started with $114. A fund of your own? Usually $10,000 or more.
It should start at $10.
