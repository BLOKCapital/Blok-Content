25 cents. That's what it took to start saving with the US Treasury in 1917. 👇

In December 1917, the Treasury launched the Thrift stamp: 25 cents each. Stick 16 on a Thrift card and you were most of the way to a $5 War Savings stamp. Kids saved pennies at school to buy them, and posters went up in shop windows everywhere.

In all, Americans put about $930 million into war savings stamps.

Saving had a 25-cent door over a hundred years ago. Owning a fund? Many mutual funds still ask for $3,000 just to start.
