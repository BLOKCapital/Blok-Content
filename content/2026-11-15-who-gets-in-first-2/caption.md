You tapped request shares. Here's who was already standing in front of you. 👇

Airbnb, December 2020. The big funds got their shares the night before, at $68. When trading opened to everyone the next morning, the first price was $146.

That's not a one-off. One study of over 1,000 IPOs found funds got about double the shares regular people got.

Keep walking up the line and it gets older. In 2009 one early backer put $20,000 into Airbnb for 6%. Before that, the founders were selling $40 cereal boxes just to keep going.

None of them got in by being faster. They owned a piece early. And in the US, investing that early usually needs $1 million in net worth (not counting your home) or a high income.
