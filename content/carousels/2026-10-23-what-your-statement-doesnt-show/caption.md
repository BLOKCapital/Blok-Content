5 things your fund statement doesn't show you (for dummies) 🌱

1. what the fund held on the days between statements
2. the trades made with your money
3. the full cost in plain money (most statements show a %, if anything)
4. who sits in between: fund house, custodian, registrar, broker or app
5. when your cash actually moved (payouts often take 1-3 business days)

none of this makes funds bad. a statement is a summary, not the full record.

the bit nobody tells you: on an on-chain portfolio, every holding, trade, cost and settlement sits on a public ledger, in an account only you control, live. it's newer tech and prices can swing, so start small and learn first.

save this for the next time your statement lands 👇

which one surprised you most?
