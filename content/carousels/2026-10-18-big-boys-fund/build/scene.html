Amsterdam · 1774

The first fund was built for people without much money.

unity is strength.
A Dutch broker, van Ketwich, pooled savings so "investors of limited means" could spread their money widely. He named it Eendragt Maakt Magt: unity is strength.
The whole idea

Put a little in. Own a slice of a lot.

that's all a fund is.
One pool, many investments. A small amount gets the same spread as a big one.
250 years later

Then the fund got a door policy.

you must be this rich.
In India, a portfolio run for you (PMS) starts at ₹50 lakh. An alternative fund (AIF), ₹1 crore. Even the new specialised funds (SIF), ₹10 lakh.
And at the door

A setup fee. A bank intro fee.

a club for the big boys.
Plenty of these funds are run well, and what they charge for running them is fair. The problem is the door: most people never get in.
