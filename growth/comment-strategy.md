# Comment growth strategy: Instagram + X (24 Oct 2026)

Full strategy (Claude Doc): https://claude.ai/artifact/4UJDGNFvX6vtixc55gtdkj

**Status:** proposed 24 Oct, waiting on Sheetal's yes to the keyword and the bot setup.

## Decisions
- **Keyword: STEPS** on every post (runner-up GUIDE; LIST, PDF, TEMPLATE, LINK rejected). One word for all posts;
  the bot sends each post's own doc.
- **Instagram end-card line:** "Want your own fund, even a $10 one? Comment STEPS." Never "fund in crypto" on Instagram.
- **Docs say $10, not $50** (product minimum 10 USDC, brand narrative).
- **Bot:** a Meta-approved comment-to-DM tool (ManyChat recommended). Composio can't send the first private-reply DM,
  which is why the 15 Oct GARDEN test only produced public replies hours later. First DM has a button, no link;
  the doc goes in the second message. Never follow-gate; ask for the follow inside the DM.
- **Two asks max in the video:** keyword + one quick question. Send line in the caption, follow ask in the DM.
- **X:** ask for answers, not keywords. Weekly action post: "reply with the amount you'd start with and we'll send
  you the 3 steps"; we answer every reply publicly with the link within 2 hours, mutuals first. No X DM automation.

## Baseline (24 Oct)
IG: 29 comments on the last 30 posts (incl. our replies), 0 on the 7 posts since 21 Oct. X: 3 to 9 replies per post.

## Targets by 7 Nov
10+ comments per Reel, 3%+ of reach comments STEPS, 60%+ tap the DM button, 5+ sends per Reel, 15+ replies per X post.
