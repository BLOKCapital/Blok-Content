Your index fund, but you can watch it work 👀

Index funds hold a basket that tracks a market and rebalance to stay on target. The catch? It all happens behind the scenes, and you find out from a factsheet later.

On-chain, the same idea runs in the open:
→ A basket with set weights
→ Rules rebalance it when prices drift
→ Every trade recorded on a public ledger
→ Running 24/7, held in an account you control

Diversifying helps, but prices can still fall, and on-chain markets are younger and swing more. Start small.
