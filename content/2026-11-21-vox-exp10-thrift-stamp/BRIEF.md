# Vox experiment reel #10 · "25 cents" Thrift stamps (Sat 21 Nov 2026, 20:30 IST)

**Variant:** H4 Number shock / S1 Single story with a twist (baseline storyline, with the other top hook) / C1 FUND at the end. Locked Vox look.

**Story.** "25 cents." December 1917: what it cost to start saving with the US Treasury. The Thrift stamp: fill a card with 16 and you were most of the way to a $5 War Savings stamp. Kids saved pennies at school. Americans put about $930M into war savings stamps. Twist: owning a fund still asks $3,000. BLOK from $10, CTA C1.
