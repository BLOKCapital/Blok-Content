In 1976, a man launched a fund that didn't even try to beat the market.
Wall Street laughed. They called it Bogle's folly.
Some called it un-American.
He hoped to raise a hundred and fifty million dollars. He got eleven.
The idea was boring. Buy the whole market. Keep costs low. Wait.
By the end of 2023, index funds held more money in America than funds run by stock pickers.
Boring won.
The next step isn't a new strategy. It's the same boring playbook, in an account only you control, on a public ledger anyone can check.
So what are we laughing at today... that we'll all be doing in fifty years?
