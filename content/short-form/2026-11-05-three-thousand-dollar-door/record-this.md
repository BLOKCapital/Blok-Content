# The $3,000 door: voiceover script (about 27 seconds)

Read it like you're telling a friend something surprising. Leave a small pause between lines.
One take on your phone is fine. Hold it close, in a quiet room.

1. In 1982, NASA sold rides to space. For three thousand dollars.
2. Not for you. For a can.
3. They called it the Getaway Special. Basically a can that rode in the shuttle's cargo bay.
4. And almost anyone could book one. Universities, companies, even high school kids.
5. About a hundred and seventy flew in twenty years.
6. So space. The most exclusive club there is. And the door was three thousand dollars.
7. Now guess what plenty of private funds and wealth managers still ask, just to let you in. *(pause here)*
8. Ten thousand dollars. Some want a hundred thousand.
9. Space got a small door forty-four years ago. Today, your own fund can start at ten dollars.
10. I wrote down the seven questions private banks ask their clients on day one. Comment FUND and I'll send them over.

Audio only or on camera both work. If you film yourself, I'll use you for the last line (the Comment FUND ask)
and run the NASA photos under the rest.
