## Facts and sources
- Thrift stamps: 25 cents, no interest; 16 on a Thrift card ($4); full card plus a few cents bought one War Savings Certificate stamp;
  WSS cost $4.12 (Dec 1917 - Jan 1918, rising 1 cent a month in 1918) and was worth $5 on 1 Jan 1923; purpose: "to allow individuals
  without the means to purchase a War Savings Certificate stamp outright". Source: Wikipedia, "War savings stamps of the United States".
- AI listing retail slice: reportedly "weighing an IPO that would reserve roughly 15%-20% of shares for individual investors", "still being
  discussed" (Bloomberg via Finimize). Company never named, no price, no buy talk. Allocations often pro-rated.
