# Instagram trial reels playbook

Researched 15 Oct 2026. Sources at the bottom; numbers from third-party guides are their testing, not Instagram's.

## How trial reels work
- A trial reel is shown **only to non-followers**. It stays off the profile grid and followers' feeds (it sits in a separate "Trials" tab on the profile) and is left out of hashtag pages and Explore during the trial.
- Distribution runs for roughly **24 to 72 hours**, in waves; early watch time decides whether it widens.
- When posting you choose: **share manually** (review after ~24h, then tap "Share to all" or leave it) or **auto-share if it performs well** (after 72h Instagram compares it with your recent content on watch time, replays, shares, saves, likes).
- Sharing manually ends the trial at once. You can't edit a trial after posting.
- The toggle is on the **last screen before Share** in the Instagram app. Needs a public professional (Business/Creator) account. Guides disagree on a follower minimum (some say 1,000+ as of 2026), so **check the toggle**; if it is missing, update the app first.
- Posting: the Instagram API supports trials (`trial_params`), but **our Composio Instagram tool doesn't expose it** (checked 15 Oct 2026), so trials are posted by hand in the app. Limit is about 20 trials a day.
- Trials get less reach than normal reels (Mosseri). **Compare trials with trials**, never with normal posts.

## The "one video, many hooks" method
1. Pick a finished reel whose body is strong (it holds attention after second 5) but whose opening could be sharper.
2. Make **5 to 7 versions** that keep the body identical and change only the first 2 to 4 seconds:
   - on-screen hook text (the biggest lever: big, top third, readable in one glance, on screen from frame 0),
   - the opening shot (start cold on a later moment, then cut to the start),
   - caption first line.
3. Make each upload visibly different so Instagram doesn't flag it as a duplicate (duplicates stall at 0 to 50 views): different hook card colour, a slight zoom (1 to 6 percent), different cold open. Caption-only changes are not enough.
4. Post them as trials **spaced 60 to 90 minutes apart on the same day or two**, so they meet similar audiences. Never post near-identical versions as normal reels: that spams followers.
5. Read results at **24h** and again at **72h** in each trial's Insights.
6. **Winner = lowest skip rate** (share of viewers who swiped away in the first 3 seconds; the cleanest hook signal). Tiebreak: average watch time, then (shares + saves) per 1,000 views. Ignore likes.
7. Promote the winner: either "Share to all" after the 72h window, or (stronger, per creators) post the winning file as a **new normal Reel** with the full caption and cover. Archive the losers.
8. Log the winning hook pattern below so the next reel starts from it. Re-trial the same video with fresh hooks no sooner than ~7 days later.

Rough view bands for a trial at 16 to 24h (one creator's testing): under 30 = flagged or dead; 100 to 300 = not landing cold; 1,000+ = good.

## BLOK rules on top
- Hooks follow `brand/instagram-strategy.md`: no crypto words, no return promises. History-to-today angles (a real dated fact connected to money now) are the house style.
- Every hook fact must be checkable and noted in the test's sheet.
- Keep the AI-content label on when posting AI video.
