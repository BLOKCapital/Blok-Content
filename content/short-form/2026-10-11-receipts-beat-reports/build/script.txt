Old money has a quiet rule.
Don't just read the report. Check the books.
Here's the report a fund sends you.
Every quarter. Heavy paper. Lovely charts.
But look closely.
It shows a single day, often weeks after it happened.
Everything in between? You take it on trust.
Now, the books.
An on-chain portfolio lives on a public ledger, a shared record no one can quietly edit.
Every trade is written down the moment it happens.
Time-stamped. Permanent. Anyone can check.
It's newer, and prices can swing. But the record is always open.
One is a report. The other, a receipt.
And receipts beat reports.
