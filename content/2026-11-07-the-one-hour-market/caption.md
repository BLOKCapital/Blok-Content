Tomorrow India's stock market opens on a Sunday, for about an hour. Here's why 👇

It's Muhurat trading, a Diwali tradition. Diwali starts a new year for many families and businesses, so the exchanges open a short special session to mark it. The Bombay Stock Exchange has held one since 1957. This year it runs Sunday 8 Nov, 2:20 to 3:30 pm India time.

The surprising part is who shows up and why. Lots of people place small token orders. Parents buy shares for their kids, and some of those shares are kept for decades. The hour isn't about the amount. It's about making a start.
