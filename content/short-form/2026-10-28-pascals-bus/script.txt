Paris, 1662. Blaise Pascal invents the bus.
Before that, you walked. Or hired a private carriage.
His idea: five sols a seat, one leaving every few minutes.
Then Paris added a rule.
No soldiers. No servants. No labourers.
Riders drifted away. The coaches faded out.
This week, a big fund launched on brand-new digital rails.
Minimum: one hundred thousand dollars. Professionals only.
New ride. Same rule.
A fund of your own should start at ten dollars.
So who gets a seat?
