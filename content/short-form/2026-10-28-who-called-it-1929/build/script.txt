1929. Three predictions. Which one aged best?
A. Irving Fisher: "Stock prices have reached what looks like a permanently high plateau."
B. Roger Babson: "Sooner or later a crash is coming."
C. John Raskob: "Everybody ought to be rich." Put $15 a month into stocks.
Two weeks later: Black Tuesday. Fisher loses his fortune.
So B wins? Not quite.
The Raskob plan, kept up through the Depression, turned about $3,700 into almost $10,000 by 1949.
No forecast needed. A fund of your own should start at $10.
