# AI presenter version (HeyGen or similar)

## 1. Avatar
- **Pick:** a casual "UGC" or "lifestyle" avatar look, not a studio/corporate one.
- **Look:** 25–35, relaxed, hoodie or plain t-shirt, sitting on a couch or at a home desk, warm window light.
- **Framing:** vertical **9:16**, head-and-shoulders, selfie-camera feel (slight handheld if the tool offers "UGC style").
- Avoid: suits, studio backdrops, trading screens, anything that looks like a finance ad.

## 2. Voice
- Warm, conversational, a little playful; not salesy, not "announcer".
- Speed: normal to slightly fast (~1.05x). Language: English.
- If the tool supports emotion tags: "friendly / casual".

## 3. Script (paste as-is; `<break>` = short pause)
```
POV: you try to sell your index fund on a Saturday. <break time="0.4s"/>
Order placed. And... it waits. <break time="0.3s"/> Till Monday.
Because markets keep office hours. Weekdays only. <break time="0.4s"/>
On-chain investing works differently. Same idea, a basket of assets, but it runs on a public ledger that never closes.
Trades settle in minutes, and you can check every holding yourself. <break time="0.4s"/>
Is it riskier? It's newer and it can swing more. So learn first. <break time="0.3s"/>
Follow for plain-English money stuff.
```
If your tool doesn't support `<break>` tags, delete them and use commas/full stops.

## 4. Export settings
- 1080×1920, MP4, 30fps. **No** captions/subtitles burned in (we add on-brand ones).
- No background music (we add it in the edit).
- Target length 27–31s.

## 5. Send back
Upload the MP4 here (or share a Drive/Dropbox link). Claude then:
1. burns in `captions.srt` in brand style and adds the on-screen text beats from `brief.md`
2. picks the cover frame and shows you the final cut for approval
3. posts it as a Reel with Instagram's **"AI info" label on** and "AI-generated presenter" in the caption

## Disclosure (required)
- Turn on Instagram's AI label when posting.
- Caption line: `AI-generated presenter.`
- The avatar must never be presented as a real customer or as someone who personally made money.
