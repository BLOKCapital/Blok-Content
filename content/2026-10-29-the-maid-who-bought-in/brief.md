# The maid who bought in (talking time-travel vlog, Part 1)

Format copied from Sheetal's reference (29 Oct 2026): an AI presenter selfie-vlogs inside a historical moment,
talking on camera with real lip sync while the scene moves around her. New scene every 8s, mono captions.

- Angle: 1602 Amsterdam, first public share sale, no minimum; a housemaid (Neeltgen Cornelis) put in 100 guilders.
  Today most funds want $10k to $100k. BLOK: your own fund from $10. Series tease: Part 2 = 1929 (posted around Black Tuesday's anniversary, 29 Oct).
- Suspense: "I came here for one name in this book" held to clip 5.
- Pipeline: presenter + scene stills with gpt_image_2_5 (Higgsfield), each clip Veo 3.1 image-to-video with native
  speech (dialogue in the prompt), 8s x 7. Assembly, captions, speech QA (faster-whisper base.en) in the Composio sandbox.
- Fact source: worldsfirststockexchange.com/2020/10/15/the-worlds-first-ipo/ (charter art. 10 open to all residents,
  no minimum or maximum, 1,143 Amsterdam subscribers, maid Neeltgen Cornelis 100 guilders).
