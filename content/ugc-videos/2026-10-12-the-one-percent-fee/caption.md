Your fund's 1% fee isn't small. 🌱

$10,000 growing 7% a year for 30 years ≈ $76,123.
Same money, same growth, minus a 1% yearly fee ≈ $57,435.
That's about $18,688 that never shows up as a bill.

A fund's running costs (the expense ratio) come out of the fund itself, a little every day. Fees vary a lot, and many index funds charge far less, so it's worth checking yours.

An on-chain portfolio works differently: every holding, every trade and every fee sits on a public record you can check yourself, any time.

Receipts beat reports.

Look up your fund's expense ratio today. It takes two minutes.
