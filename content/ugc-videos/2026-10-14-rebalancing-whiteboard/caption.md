Your 60/40 portfolio probably isn't 60/40 anymore. 🌱

Say you set it up as 60% stocks, 40% bonds. Stocks have a great year, and suddenly you're closer to 75/25. Nobody asked you. You just quietly ended up taking more risk than you planned.

The fix has a name: rebalancing. Trim what grew, top up what fell behind, and you're back to the mix you actually chose. Most people know they should. Very few ever do it.

On-chain portfolios can rebalance automatically, on rules that sit on a public record anyone can check. You don't have to remember, and you don't have to take anyone's word for it.
