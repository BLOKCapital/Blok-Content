Ambassador Program launch: team recording kit

Six backgrounds, one script each. Pick one, record yourself on your phone, drop it in. Every post should sound different: that's the whole point of the program.

1 · PICKChoose a visual below. Use 9:16 for Reels and TikTok, 16:9 for X.
2 · RECORDPhone at eye level, window light on your face, plain wall behind you. Say the script in your own words.
3 · COMBINECapCut: add the loop, add your clip as an overlay, then Remove background (or Chroma key for the green TV).
4 · POSTUse the caption, put the form link where the platform allows, and send it to Sheetal before posting.

How to put it together

    Before you post

    • Say amounts in US dollars. No return promises, no "you'll make money", no price talk.
    • On Instagram, skip crypto words (crypto, token, DeFi, wallet). On X, they're fine.
    • Form link: docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdJ6Btnwz_7dJ8Rlrkj_5d4lgAm7oJvrOW17ZzNugqv2OZZfA/viewform. On Instagram it goes in your bio or a Story link sticker, not the caption.
    • Finish with: "Apply via the link." End-card clips from the launch film are in broll/ if you want a punchy ending.
    • Send your cut to Sheetal before posting.
