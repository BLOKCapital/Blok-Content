## Caption
Your fund's 1% fee isn't small 🌱

$10,000 growing 7% a year for 30 years ≈ $76,123.
Same money, same growth, minus a 1% yearly fee ≈ $57,435.
That's about $18,688 that never shows up as a bill.

A fund's running cost (the expense ratio) comes out of the fund itself, a little every day. Fees vary a lot, and many index funds charge far less, so it's worth checking yours.

On-chain, every holding, trade and fee sits on a public record you can check yourself, any time. It's newer and prices can swing more, so learn first and start small.
