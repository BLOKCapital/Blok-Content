myth 3

"My statement shows what I own."

It shows your units. The fund's actual holdings are disclosed periodically (monthly in India, quarterly in the US) and held by a custodian for you.
On-chain flips that: you hold the assets and can check them any time. The catch: it's newer tech, and prices still swing.
