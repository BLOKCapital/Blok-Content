- Angle: Black Tuesday, 29 Oct 1929. Ordinary people did get in, but on 10% down and borrowed money; the crash wiped many out.
  Meanwhile Massachusetts Investors Trust (Boston, 1924) let ordinary people in and out at the real value of its holdings.
  Lesson: get in small, own it outright, see what you hold. Tease Part 3 = 1976 (first index fund for regular investors).
- No crypto words. No fee attacks. USD only. Risk line in caption.
- Facts: billofrightsinstitute.org/essays/the-crash-of-1929/ (10% down, rest borrowed; 16 million shares on 29 Oct;
  hundreds of thousands of new investors); mfs.com "First Fund: the origins and legacy of Massachusetts Investors Trust"
  (sell back any time at underlying value, sum within reach of ordinary people).
