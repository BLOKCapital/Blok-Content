Charlie Munger said one number is the hardest you'll ever hit.
Not a million. Not your first thousand.
The first $100,000.
"The first $100,000 is a b****,
but you gotta do it."
"After that, you can ease off the gas a little bit."
Why? At the start, every dollar comes from you.
Later, your money starts doing some of the work.
So the start is the hardest part. And most funds make it harder.
Often $10,000 just to get in.
Your first fund should start at $10.
