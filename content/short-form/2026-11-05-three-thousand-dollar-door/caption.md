In 1982, NASA sold rides to space for $3,000. Not for people. For a can.

It was called the Getaway Special: a small canister that rode in the space shuttle's cargo bay. Universities, companies and even high school students booked them, and about 170 flew between 1982 and 2003.

Space had a $3,000 door 44 years ago. Plenty of private funds and wealth managers still want $10,000 or more just to let you in. Today your own fund can start at $10.
