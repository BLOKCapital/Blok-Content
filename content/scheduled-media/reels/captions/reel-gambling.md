"Investing like this is just gambling." Is it, though? 🎰

You can gamble on a stock app too. The platform is just the plumbing. What you buy, and how long you hold it, is the strategy.

Gambling: short-term bets, chasing hype, all-in on one thing.
Investing: a diversified basket, rebalanced on rules, held for the long run.

The real red flags? "Guaranteed" returns, "get in before it's too late", borrowing to bet bigger, and money you can't afford to lose.

Still risky? Yes. Newer markets can swing hard, so diversify, start small, and learn before you leap. That's the whole point of a fund of your own, even a $10 one: a small, steady basket, not a bet.
