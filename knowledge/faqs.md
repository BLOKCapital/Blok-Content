# FAQs (content-ready answers)

**What is BLOK Capital?** A decentralized wealth-management protocol on Arbitrum. You plant a Garden (your own on-chain portfolio), then follow a curated index or manage it yourself. Your keys never leave your wallet.

**Who holds my money?** You do. Assets stay in your smart wallet. Smart contracts enforce the rules; no one, not BLOK and not a Gardener, can withdraw your funds.

**Is there a minimum?** No. You can start with as little as 10 USDC.

**Fees?** None today. Transactions are gasless. Future fee parameters would be set by DAO vote.

**Which assets?** ERC-20s supported by the protocol's oracles and rebalancer on that chain, e.g. WETH, ARB.

**Where is it available?** Worldwide. No geographic restrictions at the protocol level.

**Do I need a seed phrase?** You can sign in with Google; a smart wallet is created for you.

**What's a Gardener?** A verified manager who can trade inside your Garden under smart-contract rules but can never move funds out. Coming soon.

**Rewards?** Loyal, long-term users can receive BLOKC, which carries perks and governance power.

**Is it audited?** Contracts have been security-reviewed by CredShields, SolidityScan and Octane; a bug bounty and audit contests are announced. (Say "security-reviewed", not "audited/safe".)

**Support?** Telegram (t.me/BLOKCapital), X (@blok_cap), Farcaster, Discord.
