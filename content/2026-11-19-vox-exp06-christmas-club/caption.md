1909. A small-town bank says: bring a little every week. 👇

Carlisle Trust Company in Carlisle, Pennsylvania, opened what's known as the first Christmas club. Members paid in a small amount each week and got it all back in time for Christmas. About 350 people joined, and they saved about $28 each.

Banks everywhere copied it. By 1917, a bank in High Point, North Carolina ran a full-page ad with clubs from 1 cent a week, and one line we love: "Everybody Can Join. Nobody is Barred Out."

Saving went weekly over a century ago. Owning a fund? Many mutual funds still ask for $3,000 just to start.
