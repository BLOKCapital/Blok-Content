## The thinking
- **16 to 18 Oct is the 100-follower push:** 3 Instagram + 3 X posts a day, as Sheetal set on 15 Oct. Every slot is now filled.
- **From 19 Oct the cadence drops to what the stock can sustain:** 1 Instagram feed post a day at 18:52 IST (India evening, US morning) and 2 X posts a day (11:30 and 20:00 IST, inside the model account's 05:30 to 08:45 and 13:00 to 16:00 UTC windows). Finished stock runs out on 21 Oct, so the open slots after that are the to-make list.
- **X gets the Instagram pieces that never went to X**, rewritten in the tweet style guide (lowercase, one thought per line, ends in a question). One in three is a pure conversation post with no product mention, per the style guide.
- **Topic spacing:** the fee topic already ran 3 times (13 to 15 Oct), so the fee Reels and the fee '-A' draft are on the shelf. The 3 myths carousel overlaps the diversified Reel, so it runs Sunday as a save-it recap, three days after.
- **Keynes trial reels:** one variant every two days at 20:30 IST, by hand in the app (our API can't make trials). Each gets its 24h read before the next. Winner to the feed on 30 Oct.
- **Who posts what:** every 'scheduled' row has a one-shot scheduled task that posts through Composio and then marks the calendar row. A daily 20:15 IST check syncs what actually went live and reminds Sheetal on trial days.
