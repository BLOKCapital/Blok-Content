10 years as a customer. Not one day as an owner. 👇

Jay bought tacos at the same truck every Friday for ten years. When he found it, it was one window and a folding table. He told everyone. Now it's 300 restaurants.

Then he finds out the owner's neighbors put money in on day one. Jay never got that chance.

Big companies work the same way. For most of the 1990s, the typical company was about 9 years old when the public could first buy its shares. In 2025, it was 12. That's a lot of growing while it's mostly insiders and big money getting in.

You won't always get in first. But what you already have, you can own.
