# Compliance & Claims Checklist

BLOK Capital's brand is trust. Run every piece through this before it ships.
This is a marketing guardrail, not legal advice. Escalate anything borderline to the team.

## Hard no's
- [ ] No promised, projected or implied returns ("earn 12%", "double your money", "passive income").
- [ ] No "guaranteed", "risk-free", "safe", "can't lose", "unhackable", "fully audited".
- [ ] No price predictions or price talk about $BLOKC or any asset.
- [ ] No calls to buy $BLOKC; no IDO/presale hype, countdowns or allocation FOMO.
- [ ] No fake testimonials, fake numbers, fake user counts, or AI avatars presented as real customers.
- [ ] No naming/disparaging competitors without approval.
- [ ] No targeting minors; no gambling framing.

## Must-haves
- [ ] Every factual claim traceable to `knowledge/` or docs.blokcapital.io.
- [ ] Roadmap items worded as planned/coming/targeted (e.g. "Gardeners are coming soon").
- [ ] Performance visuals marked "Illustrative" unless they're real, dated, sourced data.
- [ ] Risk line where investing is discussed:
      *"Crypto is volatile and you can lose money. Only invest what you can afford to lose. Not financial advice."*
- [ ] Paid creators / UGC: clear disclosure (#ad, "paid partnership" label).
- [ ] AI-generated people/voices: disclose per platform rules (Meta/TikTok "AI-generated" label).
