## Instagram
How a public ledger actually works, built as a machine you can see 🌱

Every payment is a slip in a box. Entries are collected into a block, one sealed page of the ledger.
Each seal remembers the one before. The seal is a fingerprint of this page plus the previous page's seal, so the pages are coupled into one chain.
Change one page and everything after it breaks. Its seal stops matching, every link after it comes loose, and the other copies of the ledger, which still hold the original, say no.

That's why on-chain records are receipts, not reports: anyone can check them, any time, instead of waiting for a statement.

The catch: a tamper-proof record doesn't make prices calm. On-chain assets can still swing, so learn first and start small.
