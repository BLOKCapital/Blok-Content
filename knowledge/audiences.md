# Audiences & Personas

## Primary: "The Busy Grower" (Investor / Garden Owner)
- 24–45, salaried or freelance, curious about crypto, burned or scared by hype.
- Has some savings; wants exposure without day-trading or trusting an exchange with custody.
- Pain: "I don't have time to watch charts." "I don't trust exchanges after FTX." "Crypto feels like a casino."
- Wants: simple, diversified, set-and-check-monthly, transparent.
- Platforms: Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, X (lurker).
- Message: Everyone deserves a Garden. Your keys, always. Boring is a feature.

## Secondary: "The Crypto-Native Pragmatist"
- Has a wallet, has used DeFi, tired of degen culture, wants long-term structure.
- Pain: rebalancing manually, juggling 6 protocols, gas costs.
- Platforms: X, Farcaster, Telegram, Discord.
- Message: Index Gardens rebalance themselves; gasless; on-chain receipts; DAO.

## Tertiary: "The Gardener" (manager / strategist)
- Analysts, finfluencers with real strategies, small fund managers, quant hobbyists.
- Pain: no way to prove track record; can't take clients without custody/licensing overhead.
- Message: publish strategies, take clients, build an on-chain reputation nobody can fake.

## Builder (developer)
- Solidity/EVM devs, hackathon teams.
- Message: open-source MIT, EIP-2535 Diamond, facets; build on Gardens; contributor rewards.

## Global angle
No geographic restrictions. Strong resonance in emerging markets (India, Nigeria, LATAM, MENA)
where access to wealth management is gated. The core team is global. Consider localised content
(docs exist in Spanish and French).
