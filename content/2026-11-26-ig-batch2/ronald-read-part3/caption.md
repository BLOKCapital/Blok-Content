A janitor left his town $6 million. Quiet Millionaires, Part 3: the gift. 👇

Ronald Read pumped gas in Brattleboro, Vermont for about 25 years, then worked as a janitor for 17 more. He drove a used car and held his coat together with safety pins.

After he died in 2014, aged 92, his town found out:
$4.8 million went to Brattleboro Memorial Hospital.
$1.2 million went to Brooks Memorial Library.
For both, it was the biggest gift they had ever received.

For decades he bought shares in big companies he understood, and rarely sold. Like Grace Groner (Parts 1 and 2 of her story are on our page), he owned it, and he was patient.
