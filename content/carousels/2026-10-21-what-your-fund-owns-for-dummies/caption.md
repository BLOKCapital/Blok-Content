how to check what your fund actually owns (for dummies) 🌱

1. find the factsheet (search your fund's name + "factsheet")
2. read the top 10 holdings
3. put your funds side by side and look for overlap
4. turn the expense ratio into money: 1% of 10,000 is 100 a year
5. check the date on the holdings list

that last one surprises people: the list you're reading is usually weeks or months old.

the bit nobody tells you: an on-chain portfolio shows every holding live, to you, anytime, on a public ledger in an account only you control. it's newer tech and prices can swing, so start small and learn first.

save this for the next time you buy a fund 👇
