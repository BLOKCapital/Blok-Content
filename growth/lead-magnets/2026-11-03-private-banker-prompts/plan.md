# The Private Banker Prompts: comment-to-get plan (3 Nov 2026)

Replaces the $10 Fund Kit HTML (Sheetal: the hosted HTML showed a browser warning and felt like AI slop).

## What we learned from comment-to-get creators
Instagram itself is login-walled from our tools (Composio's Instagram API can't read other accounts), so this is from
the published freebie packs and keyword guides (Claude skill packs by @samdespo / Jake Schincariol / Ootto, ManyChat
guides). Patterns that get comments and shares:
1. It's a tool you use, not a read: copy-paste prompts, a skill, a template you duplicate.
2. Familiar format that opens anywhere: Google Doc / Sheet / Notion / GitHub. No unknown sites, no warnings.
3. Insider framing: "the exact system X uses", the thing normally reserved for pros.
4. A specific number in the promise ("7 prompts", "13 skills"), shown working in the video.
5. It produces something personal (your plan, your doc), so people come back and talk about it.

## The freebie
"The Private Banker Prompts": the first meeting top private banks give clients with ~$10M, as 7 copy-paste prompts
for ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. Ends with the person's own Investment Policy Statement. Bonus: paste-in instructions that
turn Claude Projects / ChatGPT into a coach that remembers their rules (our "skill" for normal people).
Brand fit: access (a $10M meeting, free), no fee talk, US dollars, no crypto words, risk line.

## Keyword and end line
Keyword: **BANKER**.
End of every video: "Top private banks only sit down with you at ten million dollars. Comment BANKER and I'll send you their first meeting, as seven prompts."

## Delivery
1. Upload the .docx to Google Drive, open with Google Docs, Share: Anyone with the link, Viewer.
2. Send the link with `/edit` swapped for `/copy`: people get a "Make a copy" button and their own doc.
3. ManyChat (or similar Meta-approved tool): comment BANKER, public reply "Sent, check your DMs", DM with button, then the link. Never follow-gate.
