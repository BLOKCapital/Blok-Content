Who actually holds your money? 🤔

When you buy a fund, your money takes a little trip: your app or broker, the fund house, a custodian that holds the actual assets, and a registrar that keeps the record of your units.

You own the units. But what you see is a statement, every month or quarter.

On-chain flips that:
→ Assets sit in an account only you control
→ Holdings visible any time, on a public ledger
→ Fewer hands in between

It's newer and it can swing more, so learn first and start small.
