## Fact check (22 Oct 2026)
- NYSE ended Saturday sessions on 29 Sep 1952, "to create a five day work-week": Emory GBSL, https://scholarblogs.emory.edu/gbsl/tdibh-nyse-ends-saturday-session
- NYSE hours before Sept 1952: weekdays 10:00–15:00, Saturdays 10:00–12:00: https://www.tradinghours.com/publications/historical-trading-hours
- Nasdaq 23/5: SEC approved early Q2 2026, projected launch 6 Dec 2026 (Q1 2026 results call, 23 Apr 2026): https://insurancenewsnet.com/oarticle/nasdaq-aims-to-debut-23-5-trading-on-6-december-2026
- 1952 → 2026 = 74 years ✓. "about to" is fair: approved, ~6 weeks out, not live.
- Session shape used in the chart (ET): Sun 21:00 → Fri 20:00 with a 20:00–21:00 break each weekday night = 115 hrs.
