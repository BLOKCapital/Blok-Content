## Headline

1. **Instagram: Reels are the only thing that reaches new people.** Reels since 15 Oct reached a median of ~160 accounts;
   carousels reached ~10 (basically our 65 followers). Carousels earned 0 follows.
2. **Instagram: people leave in the first few seconds.** Our 44 to 69 second reels hold viewers for 3.4 to 9 seconds on average
   (8 to 20%). Hooks get us distributed; the bodies lose people.
3. **X: every educational post lands at 350 to 660 views, and followers are flat** (5,770 on 13 Oct, 5,772 today).
   Only three things broke the ceiling: a real launch with an action (waitlist, 4,969 views, 34 reposts, 9 quotes),
   a text-only greentext (1,057) and an opinion walkthrough (881).
4. **X: replies cost the most effort for the least return.** 263 replies in 7 days = 8,435 views total (median 20 each),
   about the same as two normal posts. Replies under bigger finance/AI accounts got 180 to 386 views; replies to the same
   small ring of accounts (AmeliaonAI, ai_elon_musk_, btxFYI, Zuriwq59b, VeyraWithAI…) get ~10. One-liners
   ("Yes very true", "Lovely", "Come and join now") were still going out on 17 Oct.

## Changes (what we do from Wed 21 Oct)

### Instagram
1. **Reels first: 1 Reel a day, carousels at most 2 a week.** Every carousel idea also ships as a 20 to 30s Reel.
2. **Cut reels to 20 to 30 seconds.** The twist goes in the first 2 seconds as on-screen text *and* voiceover
   (e.g. "wall street laughed at this fund. it now holds more money than the stock pickers"), not the setup.
   Goal: average watch time over 10s.
3. **Give a reason to send it.** End every reel and caption with a "send this to the friend who…" line or a one-word
   question ("would you have bought it in 1976? yes or no"). Shares are the metric to beat (currently 0 to 2).
4. **Space reels at least 4 hours apart**; never two within the same hour.
5. **Keep the history-fact hook pattern**, connect it to "everyone can have a fund, even a $10 one" in the last line.
6. Trial reels: keep the schedule; judge on skip rate and avg watch, not reach (trial 3 is only 6h old).

### X
1. **One stance post a day** (greentext, translation joke, unpopular opinion, poll-in-words), text only, no product.
   It's the format that travelled furthest outside the launch.
2. **History posts: swap collage art for a receipt image** (headline, letter, chart) and end with a forced choice,
   not a lesson.
3. **One "action" post a week** with a clear thing to do (waitlist invite, "build your $10 fund"), since action posts are
   the only ones that got reposts and quotes at scale.
4. **Replies: fewer, bigger, earlier.** About 15 a day, not 40. Target posts from 10k to 500k-follower personal-finance,
   investing and fintech accounts within 30 minutes of posting, each reply carrying a number, a counter-example or a
   specific question. Max 1 reply a day to the same small-account ring. No one-liners, ever.
5. Followers are flat: pin the waitlist launch post so profile visitors have a reason to follow.

### Follower growth (Sheetal, 20 Oct: "focus deeply on engagement, we want more followers")
Neither account converts attention into follows yet (IG 65 followers, 0 follows recorded on any carousel; X flat at ~5,770).
A view only becomes a follow when the viewer expects *more of the same* from us. So:
1. **Make series, not one-offs.** Name a recurring series and number it on screen and in line 1:
   IG "money history, part 7" Reels; X "money history" posts on the same days each week. "Part 1 of 3" endings
   ("part 2 tomorrow: the day wall street closed on wednesdays") give a reason to follow tonight.
2. **Pin three best posts on both profiles** (IG: the 1-customer, Bogle and Keynes reels; X: waitlist launch + the
   greentext + the best history post) so a profile visitor sees the series in one glance.
3. **Bio does one job:** "money history, explained in 30 seconds. everyone deserves a fund, even a $10 one."
4. **Answer every comment within the first hour** on IG and every reply in the first 2 hours on X, with a question back.
   Comment threads are the cheapest engagement signal we have, and replying doubles the count.
5. **On X, spend reply time where new people are**: under 10k to 500k-follower finance/investing accounts' fresh posts,
   not inside our own small ring. Our best replies there pulled 180 to 386 views each vs ~10 in the ring.
6. **Every Reel/post ends with a follow reason**, not a CTA to the product: "follow for one forgotten money story a day".
