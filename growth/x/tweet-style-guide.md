# Tweet style guide (how we write every X post)

**Read this before writing any X post, reply or thread, right after `growth/x/x-algorithm-playbook.md`.**
The playbook decides *what X rewards* (copy-link shares, replies, quotes, no reports). This guide decides
*how the words look and feel*. If they ever disagree, the playbook wins, then `brand/compliance.md`.

Model account: **@FoxyhitsW** ("Foxy", ~6.4K followers, ghostwriter for crypto brands), picked by Sheetal.
Data: all 20 of their original posts from 8 to 15 Oct 2026, pulled with @blok_cap's X API access, plus older posts
found by search. We copy the **thinking, structure and formatting**, never their sentences or topics.

## Why this account is worth copying

- Median post: **~3,200 impressions on 6.4K followers**, so most posts travel well beyond the follower base.
- **Replies are about half of likes** (1,829 replies vs 3,614 likes across 19 posts, excluding the one outlier).
  Under the playbook a reply is worth 5 and a like 0.5, so this account's engine is exactly the one X now pays for.
- Best post of the week: **394K impressions, 197 bookmarks, 45 quotes** (a scandal plus a screenshot as proof).
- Our own last original posts: 7 replies on 18 likes at best. The gap is the writing, not the topics.

## The 10 rules

### Formatting (how it looks)
1. **lowercase by default.** Sentences start lowercase. Keep capitals only where meaning needs them
   (a ticker, an acronym like ETF or SEC, a brand that looks wrong otherwise). It reads like a person texting, not a press release.
2. **One thought per line, blank line between lines.** Never a paragraph. A post is 3 to 6 short lines with air around them.
3. **No full stop at the end of a line.** Line breaks do the punctuation. Commas only when the line would misread without one.
4. **Short words.** "rn", "imo", "tbh", "ngl" are fine once per post. No corporate words (leverage, ecosystem, unlock, empower, revolutionize).
5. **Lists use `>` or `-`, never numbers with full sentences.** Each item 2 to 6 words. Lists go after a colon on a hook line.
6. **0 or 1 emoji, at the end of a line, never decorative.** It carries tone the words can't (😭 for self-mockery, 💀 for disbelief, 👇 when the list or link is below). No 🚀, no 🌱 chains, no hashtags.
7. **The image is the receipt.** When there's a screenshot, chart or document, the text sets it up in 1 to 2 lines and the image delivers. No stock art.

### Thinking (why people reply)
8. **Line 1 must work alone.** It is the whole tweet for 80% of readers. It's one of the hook types below, never a greeting, never "we're excited".
9. **Every post ends with a reason to reply or send it.** Either a forced choice ("W or L?", "left or right?", "be honest"), an
   open question about the reader ("which one are you?"), or a short verdict line people want to argue with or agree with in public.
10. **Talk to one person, from one person.** "you" and "i/we", never "users" or "investors". Admit things (a loss, a mistake, a
    surprise). Confidence with a wink, never a lecture.

## The hook types (pick one per post)

| # | Hook type | Shape | What it triggers |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Specific number + time box** | `[number] [thing] in [short period]` then restate it more bluntly on its own line | Awe, bookmarks, "wait what" quotes |
| 2 | **"so apparently…" receipt** | Something surprising just happened + screenshot. Second line scales it ("imagine how many…") | Copy-link shares and quotes; their best post |
| 3 | **Translation joke** | `[institution]: "[what they say]"` / `translation: "[what it really means]"` | Recognition; people send it to a friend |
| 4 | **Ladder** | `[thing] at each level:` then 5 to 10 lines from small to big, each a tiny joke | Saves; people reply with their own rung |
| 5 | **Not as hard as you think** | Bold claim, then the plain arithmetic that proves it, then a one-line verdict | Debate replies (agree and disagree both count) |
| 6 | **If you could only…** | `if you had to pick one [x] for the next year, make it [y]` + one line of proof | Bookmarks, "why?" replies |
| 7 | **Confession** | One short line of vulnerability + one line asking for help or opinion | Empathy replies, high reply count |
| 8 | **Equation / contrast** | `what we expect:` vs `what actually happens:` laid out as two mini blocks, kicker "few understand" style | Quotes, "this is so true" replies |
| 9 | **Identity filter** | `if your [friends / portfolio / bank] [does x]:` + list + "cherish that" style verdict | Tribe signalling; people tag friends |
| 10 | **Binary poll in words** | Show two options (images or lines), ask "left or right?" / "W or L?" | Cheapest replies there are; use 1 in 5 posts max |

## Shapes (copy the skeleton, fill in our substance)

**Number post (hook 1)**
```
[big number] [what happened] [in what period]

[the same fact said more bluntly]

[one line on why it matters to the reader's money]

[forced-choice or open question]
```

**Translation post (hook 3)**
```
[institution]: "[their polite line]"

translation: "[the honest version, long and a bit absurd]"
```

**Long post (over 280 chars, use for advice or how-to)**
```
[promise line ending in a colon]

[one line of why you're qualified / who asked]

most people get it wrong because:

- [mistake]
- [mistake]
- [mistake]

here's what works:

- [step]
- [step]
- [step]

[one-line verdict]
```

## What we do NOT copy

These work for a personal account and would break our brand, compliance or the playbook:
- **Profanity and insults** ("mfs", calling groups scammers or worse). We pick fights with systems, not people (`brand/voice.md`).
- **Income or payout flexes** ("got paid $X", "make $10k/month"). That is a return promise under `brand/compliance.md`.
- **Tagging a company to call it out.** The playbook bans tagging accounts that don't know us; it invites blocks and reports (−234).
- **"delete posts that flop and repost them".** The model account recommends it; our playbook says never delete and repost. Playbook wins.
- **Personal off-topic bait** (height, relationships). Our relatable posts stay inside money, saving, investing and work.
- **Token talk, price talk, memecoin topics.** Same compliance rules as always; X can say "on-chain", Instagram can't.

## Before / after

### 1. The night a bank vanished (`content/2026-10-12-the-night-a-bank-vanished/tweet.md`)

**Before** (capitalised, a date as the hook, the payoff is a brand slogan, no reason to reply):
```
9 March 2023: customers asked Silicon Valley Bank for $42 billion back. In one day.

By morning, the bank was shut down.

A bank balance is a promise. An on-chain portfolio sits in an account only you control, on a public ledger anyone can check.

Receipts beat reports. 🌱
```

**After, hook 1 (number + time box)**, attach the Fed testimony screenshot as the receipt:
```
$42 billion tried to leave one bank in a single day

by the next morning the bank didn't exist

your balance was never cash in a drawer, it was a promise

would you rather hold a promise or a receipt?
```
Why it's better: the number leads, line 2 is the shock in plain words, line 3 is the one idea, and the last line is a
forced choice anyone can answer in one word (replies = 5). Sources and the risk line stay in our first reply, as before.

**After, hook 3 (translation)**, same story, built to be sent to a friend:
```
banks: "your money is safe with us"

translation: "your money is safe with us as long as nobody else asks for theirs on the same day as you"
```

## 30-second check before posting

- [ ] Line 1 is a hook type from the table and makes sense alone
- [ ] lowercase, one thought per line, blank lines between, no full stops at line ends
- [ ] 3 to 6 lines (or a long post using the long shape), 0 to 1 emoji, no hashtags
- [ ] Ends in a forced choice, a question about the reader, or an arguable verdict
- [ ] Image, if any, is a receipt (screenshot, chart, document)
- [ ] Nothing from "What we do NOT copy"
- [ ] Playbook checklist done (`growth/x/x-algorithm-playbook.md`) and compliance clear (risk line and sources in the first reply)
- [ ] Plan to reply to every reply for the first 2 hours: the model account's reply counts only happen because they answer back

## Rhythm

The model account posts **~3 original posts a day** and mixes them: roughly a third relatable/community questions,
a third opinion or advice, the rest news-with-receipt and personal moments. Posts land around **05:30 to 08:45 UTC**
and **13:00 to 16:00 UTC**. For @blok_cap, start with 2 a day (morning + afternoon UTC) and keep one of them a pure
conversation post (hooks 7, 8, 9 or 10) with no product mention at all.

## Lessons from our own posts (20 Oct 2026)

- History posts get replies and bookmarks but don't travel. End them on a **forced choice or a stance**, never on a lesson,
  and make line 1 the twist ("wall street laughed at this fund. it now holds more than the stock pickers"), not the date.
- Run history as a **named, numbered series** ("money history #7") so people follow for the next one.
- Greentext and "unpopular opinion" shapes are our best non-launch formats so far. Use one a day, text only.
