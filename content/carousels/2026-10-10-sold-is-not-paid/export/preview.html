Point 2 · The proof
03 / 05
T+1

That second step takes a business day, at least.

US and Indian stock and ETF trades settle the next business day. Fund redemptions often take 1 to 3. Weekends don't count.

Sources: SEC T+1 rule (May 2024) · SEBI T+1 (Jan 2023)

On-chain, the deal is the settlement.

Trade and transfer happen together, on a public ledger, any day of the week. The catch: prices swing more and the tech is newer. Start small.
