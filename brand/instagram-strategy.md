# Instagram Strategy (@blok.capital)

**This file overrides general brand guidance for everything made for Instagram:
carousels, Reels, UGC videos, Stories and captions.**

## Who we're talking to
People who already invest the traditional way: **mutual funds, ETFs, index funds,
SIPs, retirement accounts, brokerage portfolios.** They're sensible, long-term savers.
They aren't crypto people, and many are sceptical of crypto.

## What we're doing
**Pure education, as a gentle nudge.** Each piece teaches one idea:
- **why on-chain finance is good** (transparency, ownership, speed, access, always-on markets)
- **why building a portfolio on-chain is great** (you hold it, see it and verify it yourself)
- **why it matters** for someone who already invests in funds

We're not selling, and it isn't crypto content. The goal is "huh, I didn't know that,"
then a follow, then a save. Trust comes first; the product comes much later.

## The no-crypto rule
| Don't use | Use instead |
|---|---|
| crypto, coins, tokens, altcoins, Bitcoin/ETH talk | on-chain assets, digital assets (sparingly), "the asset" |
| DeFi, Web3, blockchain jargon, gas, smart contract (unexplained) | on-chain finance, public ledger, "code-enforced rules" |
| wallet, seed phrase, keys (unexplained) | "an account only you control" |
| $BLOKC, DAO, token, airdrop, IDO | never on Instagram |
| moon, pump, degen, HODL, WAGMI, charts and candles | never |
| Garden / Gardener / Index Garden as headline concepts | plain words first ("your on-chain portfolio"); "Garden" at most as a soft brand touch |

Visuals: no coin icons, ₿/Ξ symbols, candlestick charts, rockets, or neon "crypto" aesthetics.
Use the brand's calm editorial look: linen, indigo, lime, plain-English typography.

## How we talk
- Start from **what they already know**: "You know how an index fund works? Same idea, new plumbing."
- **Compare fairly and respectfully.** Funds and ETFs are good products; we're showing a new way,
  not trashing the old one. No "your fund manager is ripping you off."
- **Concrete over abstract:** settlement times, how often holdings are disclosed, market hours,
  who sits between you and your money.
- **Always be honest about the trade-offs:** newer technology, volatility, a learning curve.
  An honest "the catch" slide builds more trust than hype.
- One idea per post. Plain English. Garden metaphor is fine as warmth, never as jargon.

## Content pillars for Instagram
| Pillar | Example topics |
|---|---|
| **TradFi vs on-chain, side by side** | settlement (days vs minutes), market hours vs 24/7, periodic statements vs live visibility, layers of middlemen |
| **Ownership** | who actually holds your fund units, custodians and registrars explained, "an account only you control" |
| **Transparency** | what a public ledger is, receipts vs reports, verifying instead of trusting |
| **Familiar concepts, new rails** | index investing on-chain, automatic rebalancing you can watch, diversification |
| **Myth-busting (gently)** | "on-chain = gambling", "it's only for tech people", "you need a lot of money" |
| **Honest risks** | volatility, tech risk, scams to avoid, how to start small and learn first |

## Fact rules for TradFi comparisons
Keep claims general and true across markets. Say "typically" or "often" where practices vary
(e.g. US vs India).
- Stock/ETF trades in the US and India settle **T+1** (next business day); fund redemptions often take 1–3 business days.
- Mutual funds disclose full holdings **periodically** (monthly in India, quarterly in the US); ETFs often daily.
- Exchanges run on **weekdays, set hours**; on-chain markets run **24/7**.
- Funds involve intermediaries (AMC/fund house, custodian, registrar/transfer agent, broker/distributor).
- Never compare returns or imply on-chain portfolios earn more.
