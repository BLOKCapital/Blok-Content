# Board · Who Gets In First? Part 2 (one camera pulling back)

Built with the video-decompiler method (Linden style: one continuous move outward through nested worlds, no cut), rendered with our own 9:16 pipeline because the skill's renderer needs a GPU.

**One sentence.** You tapped request shares, the camera pulls back, and you see everyone who got in before you, all of them inside the building because they owned a piece early.

**The world.** An IPO queue seen from above, crossed with a building's floor plan with the roof off. Outside: the public, each with a glowing phone. Inside: rooms that light up one by one, from the door inward (big funds, early backers, founders). Colours: phone blue = the public, warm lamp light = already inside, coral = you, lime = your own fund.

**Moves.** The turn of scale (a phone becomes a person becomes a line becomes a building becomes a city). The answered opening (who's in front of you? everyone who owned early). Two rooms, one door (the closed door between the line and the rooms, tying back to Part 1's door).
