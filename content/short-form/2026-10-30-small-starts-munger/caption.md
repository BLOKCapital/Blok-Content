Charlie Munger said one number is the hardest you'll ever hit. Guess it before the reveal 👇

It isn't a million. It's the first $100,000. His words: "The first $100,000 is a b****, but you gotta do it." And: "After that, you can ease off the gas a little bit."

Why? At the start, every dollar comes from you. Later, your money starts doing some of the work.

So the start is the hardest part. And many funds make it harder: often $10,000 or more just to let you in. We think your first fund should start at $10.
