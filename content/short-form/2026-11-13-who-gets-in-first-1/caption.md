For 53 years you could buy the car, but not a piece of the company. 👇

Ford was founded in 1903 and stayed private until 17 January 1956. When it finally sold shares to the public, it was the largest IPO in American history up to that time, sold through a syndicate of over 200 banks.

All the years of growing up before that happened behind a closed door.

Seventy years later, one of the biggest AI companies is letting people request shares in its IPO through brokerage apps. Its own page says it plainly: "There is no guarantee that any investor will receive all or any of the shares they apply for."
