## Instagram
Up to $10 trillion of stocks, bonds and funds could live on-chain by 2030. That's Morgan Stanley talking, not a startup. 🌱

Today it's roughly $40 billion. Morgan Stanley's head of digital asset strategy put the 2030 range at $3 trillion to $10 trillion this week. The bank's own published base case, a month earlier, was $2.3 trillion.

Why banks care: on-chain records promise less paperwork, simpler transfers and assets that are easier to borrow against. First in line: private shares, structured notes and investment funds.

The part nobody says: on-chain doesn't automatically mean yours. A firm can still hold the record for you and send you a statement. The bigger shift is owning it in an account only you control, and checking it yourself.

The catch: it's a forecast, not a promise, and on-chain assets can still swing in price. Learn first, start small.
