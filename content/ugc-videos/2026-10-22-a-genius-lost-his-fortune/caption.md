The year a genius lost his fortune. 🌱

London, 1720. Isaac Newton sold his South Sea Company shares in April and took a profit. Then he watched his friends keep getting richer, and in June, near the top, he bought back in with almost all his savings. By September the price had collapsed. By mid-1721 his net worth had fallen from just over £30,000 to about £20,000. The famous story says he lost £20,000; his own records suggest closer to £10,000. Still, a third of everything he had.

The simple lesson: even the smartest person in the room chased a hot tip, because nobody could see what was really underneath. You don't need to be cleverer. Be more boring: spread it out, keep costs low, and own what you can check. Today an on-chain portfolio sits on a public ledger, so you can see every holding and every move yourself. It's newer and prices can swing, so start small and learn first.
