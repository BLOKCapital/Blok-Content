# Instagram Reel lessons (from analytics, 20 Oct 2026)

Full review: `growth/analytics/2026-10-20-analytics-review.md`.
1. Reels are our only reach (median ~160 accounts vs ~10 for carousels). 1 Reel a day; carousels max 2 a week, each also cut as a Reel.
2. History-fact hooks get distributed; keep them. But avg watch is 3.4 to 9s on 44 to 69s reels. **Make them 20 to 30s** and put the twist on screen + in the voice in the first 2s.
3. End with a reason to send ("send this to the friend who…") or a one-word question, plus "follow for one forgotten money story a day".
4. Number the series ("money history, part 7") so viewers follow for the next one.
5. Never post two reels within 4 hours of each other.
6. Reply to every comment within the first hour, with a question back.
7. (24 Oct) Every Reel ends with "Want your own fund, even a $10 one? Comment STEPS." and has its own STEPS doc. See `growth/comment-strategy.md`.
