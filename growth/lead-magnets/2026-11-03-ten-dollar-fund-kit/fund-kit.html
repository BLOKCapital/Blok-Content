    Your 3 real-life steps

    1. Learn the idea. A fund is just a basket of investments. On-chain, the basket sits in an account only you control, and every holding is visible any time, not just in a monthly statement.
    2. Get on the list. Join the BLOK waitlist at blokc.app. Sign in with Google, no forms. We let people in step by step.
    3. Start with $10. When you're in, put in an amount you'd be fine losing, watch how it behaves for a few weeks, and only then decide whether to add more.
    The honest catch

    This is newer technology, prices can move a lot, and there's a learning curve. Funds and ETFs are good products, and this isn't a reason to sell them. It's a new door, so start small and learn first.
