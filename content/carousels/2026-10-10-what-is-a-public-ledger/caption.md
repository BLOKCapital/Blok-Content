What is a public ledger, really? 📒

Picture a notebook everyone shares. Everyone holds the same copy, new entries get added, and old ones can't be quietly rubbed out.

That's the difference between a report and a receipt:
→ A report is made after the fact and summarised for you
→ A receipt is created as it happens, and anyone can verify it

On a public ledger you don't have to take anyone's word for what you own. You can check.

Transparent isn't risk-free, though. Prices can still swing, so learn first and start small.
