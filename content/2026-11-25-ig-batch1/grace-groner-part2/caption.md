Three shares. Never sold. $7 million. Part 2 of Grace's story. 👇

Part 1 recap: Grace Groner was a secretary. In 1935 she paid $180 for three shares of the company she worked for, $60 each.

So how did $180 become $7 million? Three things:
1. The company split its shares many times, so her three turned into far more.
2. She put the payouts (dividends) back in, to buy more.
3. She waited. 75 years.

The whole time she lived in a small cottage and bought clothes at rummage sales. When she died in 2010, aged 100, she left $7 million to her old college, Lake Forest College, to help students get ready for careers.

Her story is rare. The habit isn't: start small, own it, be patient.
