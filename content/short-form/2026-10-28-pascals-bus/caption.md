The first bus ever had one rule. 🚌

Paris, 18 March 1662. Blaise Pascal's idea goes live: coaches for eight people, running on fixed routes every few minutes, five sols a seat. Before that you walked, or you hired a private carriage.

Then the Parlement of Paris added a rule: no soldiers, no pages, no footmen, no labourers, "to assure the greater comfort" of the bourgeois. The fare went up, riders drifted away, and within a few years the coaches faded out.
