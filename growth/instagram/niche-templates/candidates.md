# Niche scan: candidate accounts (27 Oct 2026)

Goal: find 10 to 15 small/mid accounts in our niche whose **top posts did well organically**,
then turn each top post into a reusable template (`templates.md`).

## Filters
- Niche: personal finance, investing, funds/ETFs/SIPs, money history, economics explainers, on-chain/RWA explainers.
- Size: roughly 5k to 350k followers. Skip giants whose reach is just follower count.
- Organic only: skip posts tagged #ad / paid partnership / "sponsored", collabs with brands, and giveaways.
- Signal: a post counts as a winner when its views are **3x or more the account's median** for its last ~36 posts,
  or its likes + comments are 3x or more the median. This is relative to the account itself, so a boosted
  follower count doesn't help.
