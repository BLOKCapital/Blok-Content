# BLOK Capital — Visual Identity

Source: https://docs.blokcapital.io/en/resources/brand-guidelines/color-palette
(Full brand kit lives in the team's Figma brand guidelines file; ask for the link when needed.)

## Colour palette
| Token | Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--sky` | Sky Blue | `#7DD2FD` | Primary highlight; from the logo. UI accents, focal illustration elements. |
| `--indigo` | Deep Indigo | `#004EBA` | Depth and credibility. Headlines on light backgrounds, dark slides. |
| `--lime` | Lime Green | `#7ED116` | Growth and momentum (the Garden). Action, positive numbers, sprouts. |
| `--linen` | Neutral Linen | `#F6F3E8` | Default background ("paper"). Calm, editorial. |
| `--coral` | Coral Red | `#EC464D` | Use sparingly: CTA, one emphasis per piece, "the problem" moments. |
| `--earth` | Earth Brown | `#844947` | Outlines, shadows, structural lines, soil. |
| `--ink` | Ink (derived) | `#0B1B2B` | Body text on linen. Logo outline colour is ~`#03222A`. |

Logo mark uses a gradient from electric blue (~`#1A00FF`) to cyan (~`#00E5FF`) with
Sky Blue highlight edges.

### Pairings that work
- Linen bg + Ink text + Indigo headline + Lime accent (default, editorial)
- Indigo bg + Linen text + Sky accent (bold/statement slides)
- Ink/near-black bg + Sky + Lime (tech/builder content)
- Coral only as a single highlight: one word, one button, one arrow.

### Contrast notes
Sky Blue and Lime on Linen fail WCAG for body text; use them for shapes, highlights,
or large display text with an Ink outline, never for small copy.

## Typography
Website uses a modern sans + editorial serif mix. For content, default to:
- **Display / headlines:** `Fraunces` (editorial serif, garden-warm) or `Space Grotesk` (tech)
- **Body:** `Inter`
- **Numbers / code / addresses:** `JetBrains Mono`
All available on Google Fonts. Swap if the official brand kit specifies otherwise.

## Logo
Files in `brand/assets/`:
- `logo-mark-white-bg.png`: mark on white
- `logo-mark-black-bg.png`: mark on black
- `logo-wordmark.png`: horizontal "BLOK Capital" lockup (transparent)

Rules: don't recolour, stretch, rotate, add effects, or place on busy backgrounds.
Keep clear space ≥ the width of the inner square on all sides.

## Illustration & imagery
- Flat/editorial illustration with garden motifs (sprouts, plots, watering cans, trellises)
  meeting blockchain motifs (blocks, nodes, receipts).
- Earth Brown outlines, soft Linen grounds, Lime for growth.
- Photography (UGC): natural light, real people, real homes/desks/phones. No stock "trader
  with 6 monitors", no Lambos, no gold coins with ₿ on them.

## Formats
| Format | Size | Safe zone |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram carousel | 1080×1350 (4:5) | 60px margin; keep text out of bottom 120px on slide 1 |
| Instagram/TikTok/Shorts video | 1080×1920 (9:16) | Keep text within central 1080×1420; avoid top 220 / bottom 380 |
| X / LinkedIn image | 1600×900 (16:9) or 1080×1080 | 60px margin |
| Story | 1080×1920 | Top 250 / bottom 250 clear |
