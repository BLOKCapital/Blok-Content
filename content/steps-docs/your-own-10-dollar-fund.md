## The story in 30 seconds
For most of history, having a fund of your own was for the big boys. You needed $10,000 to $100,000
to get in the door, plus setup fees and an introduction from a bank. Everyone else could buy into
someone else's fund, but never run their own.

## What it means for you
That door is open now. With on-chain finance, a fund can be a portfolio that sits in an account only
you control, where you can see every holding at any time. It can start at $10.

## Your own $10 fund in 3 steps
1. **Learn the idea.** A fund is just a basket of investments. On-chain, the basket sits in an account
   only you control, and every holding is visible any time.
2. **Get on the list.** Join the BLOK waitlist at blokc.app: sign in with Google, no forms. We let
   people in step by step.
3. **Start with $10.** When you're in, put in an amount you'd be fine losing, watch how it behaves for
   a few weeks, and only then decide whether to add more.

## The honest catch
This is newer technology, prices can move a lot, and there is a learning curve. Start small and learn first.
