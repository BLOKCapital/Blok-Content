# Vox experiment reel #6 · The first Christmas club (Thu 19 Nov 2026, 20:30 IST)

**Variant:** H6 Cold open (starts inside the story, no setup) / S1 Single story with a twist / C1 FUND at the end. Locked Vox look. Target 30 to 36 s.

**Story.** Cold open: "1909. A small-town bank says: bring a little every week." Get it all back in time for Christmas.
About 350 people join, saving about $28 each (Carlisle Trust Company, Carlisle PA). Banks everywhere copy it, some from
1 cent a week; a 1917 ad says "Everybody Can Join. Nobody is Barred Out." Twist: owning a fund still asks $3,000 to start.
BLOK from $10. CTA C1.
