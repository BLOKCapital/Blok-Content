At 11, he sold his first stock too early. Can you guess who before the reveal? 👇

1942: three shares, $114. They dropped to $27, he held on, sold at $40 for a few dollars of profit... then watched them climb past $200 a share.

That kid was Warren Buffett. The lesson he took from it is the one his partner Charlie Munger later put best: "The big money is not in the buying and the selling, but in the waiting."

And John Bogle, the man who made index funds popular: "Don't look for the needle in the haystack. Just buy the haystack!"

Every great investor learned on a small first try. Buffett started with $114. A fund of your own usually asks for $10,000 or more. We think it should start at $10.
