# The $10 Fund Kit: comment-to-get plan (3 Nov 2026)

Freebie: `fund-kit.html` (one self-contained file, works offline, phone-first).
Status: built, waiting on Sheetal's yes. Nothing posted.

## Why this freebie (angle chosen)
Options considered: (1) "Fund door time machine" history page, (2) "$10 fund builder" with a shareable factsheet,
(3) birth-year card ("what a fund cost the year you were born"), (4) a plain 3-steps PDF (the 24 Oct STEPS doc),
(5) a factsheet-decoder guide. Picked a mix of 1 + 2: history hook (1774 to today), a "which doors open for you"
slider, a fund builder with a rebalancing demo, and a personal factsheet image people can post and tag us in.
The factsheet is the share loop; the history is our brand pattern; it lands the $10 access narrative without fee talk.

## Keyword
**FUND** for this kit (the 24 Oct plan proposed STEPS for per-post docs; FUND is more concrete for this one).

## End of every video (2 to 3 seconds)
Spoken: "Want your own fund, even a ten-dollar one? Comment FUND and I'll send you the kit."
On screen: the sample factsheet + "Comment FUND". Caption first line repeats it.
Voice: Brooks (Chatterbox) by default. Better: a one-time 3-second clip of a real team member saying the line, reused on every video.

## How people receive it (Meta-approved comment-to-DM tool, ManyChat recommended)
Composio can't send the first private reply, so it needs ManyChat (or similar) connected to @blok.capital.
1. Someone comments FUND (also match "fund", "Fund 🌱").
2. Public reply, rotating: "Sent, check your DMs 🌱" / "On its way to your inbox" / "Done! Tell us your fund's name".
3. DM 1: "Here's your $10 Fund Kit 🌱 Tap below and I'll send the link." [button: Send me the kit]
4. DM 2 (after tap): "Here you go: <link>?src=ig. Design your fund, save your factsheet, post it and tag @blok.capital. We reshare the best names."
5. DM 3 (same day): "If you liked it, follow @blok.capital for one forgotten money story a day." Never follow-gate.
