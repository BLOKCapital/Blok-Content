# X ranking playbook (Oct 2026)

**Read this before drafting any X post, reply or thread.** It overrides older X advice in this repo
(including `growth/x/what-goes-viral.md`) wherever they disagree.

Source: Sheetal's read of X's open-source ranking code after its big algorithm change (Oct 2026).
Context: 9M views in the 3 months before the change. Numbers below are copied exactly from those notes.

## How X scores a post

| Action | Weight |
|---|---|
| Copy-link share | **20** |
| Reply | 5 |
| Quote | 5 |
| DM share | 5 |
| Follow | 4 |
| Repost | 1 |
| Like | 0.5 |
| Video open | 0.07 (watch time counts for nothing) |

| Negative feedback | Weight |
|---|---|
| Report | **−234** |
| Mute | −58.8 |
| "Not interested" | −47.5 |
| Block | −31.2 |

## The rules

1. **Likes barely move the score.** Write things people copy the link to, argue with, or send to a friend.
2. **The first 2 hours decide everything.** After 48 hours a post drops out of For You completely.
   Post when the audience is online, then stay in the replies.
3. **Build mutual follows.** When you reply to a mutual follow inside your own thread, that reply gets an
   extra +15, for 20 total, the same as a copy-link share. Reply to the people who follow you back.
4. **Don't delete and repost to fix typos.** Use edit.
5. **Images aren't penalized, but they should carry information.** Real scenes, screenshots and proof-style
   images work better, and multi-photo posts keep people on the post longer.
6. **Video: watch time currently counts for nothing.** Only opening a video counts (0.07). Don't force video
   for the algorithm.
7. **Ordinary links aren't penalized (anymore).** Only malicious links are removed.
8. **Avoid negative feedback** (report −234, mute −58.8, "not interested" −47.5, block −31.2). Tagging strangers
   to ride a trend and clickbait headlines are the easiest ways to trigger them.
9. **Replies are scored by Grok from 0 to 3**, and low-scoring replies sink to the bottom of the thread.
   Low-effort replies don't just fail to help, they get buried.

## How we apply this (checklist for every X post or reply)

Before drafting:
- [ ] **Check the watchlist first** (`growth/x/watchlist.md`, page: `growth/x/watchlist/watchlist.html` in the shared folder): read the recent posts of those accounts and pick the topic from them, ideally as a quote post of one of their fresh posts (under 2 hours old is best, never over 48 hours).
- [ ] Pick the action we want: **copy-link share** first, then reply / quote / DM share. A post that only earns likes has failed.
- [ ] Plan the posting time for when our audience is online, and block the next 2 hours to answer replies.

While drafting a post:
- [ ] Give people a reason to save or send the link: a reference-grade number, a checklist, a clear explainer, a receipt.
- [ ] Give people something to reply to: a real question, a position they can disagree with, a counter-example. Never bait.
- [ ] Images carry information (screenshot, chart, proof, real scene). Use several photos when there's more than one thing to show. No decorative stock.
- [ ] Use video only when the story needs it, not for reach. A video open is worth 0.07.
- [ ] A link is fine in the main post if it helps; no need to hide it in a reply.
- [ ] No tagging accounts that don't know us, no clickbait headline, nothing that promises more than the post delivers.
- [ ] Brand and compliance still apply (`brand/voice.md`, `brand/compliance.md`): no price talk, no return promises.

While drafting a reply:
- [ ] Add substance a Grok 0–3 score would reward: a fact, a number, a counter-example, a specific question, a useful link.
- [ ] Never templated ("love this, let's connect", "would love to have you in our network"). These score low and get buried.
- [ ] Prioritise replying to mutual follows in our own threads (+15 bonus, 20 total).

After posting:
- [ ] Stay in the replies for the first 2 hours; answer everyone with real content, mutuals first.
- [ ] Found a typo? Edit it. Never delete and repost.
- [ ] Don't keep pushing a post after 48 hours; it has left For You. Make a new one instead.

## What our own numbers say (analytics review, 20 Oct 2026)

Full review: `growth/analytics/2026-10-20-analytics-review.md`.
- Educational posts land at 350 to 660 views however they're made; only **stance posts** (greentext, unpopular opinion)
  and **action posts** (the waitlist launch: 4,969 views, 9 quotes) broke out. Plan one stance post a day, one action post a week.
- Text-only greentext (1,057) beat every image post. Collage art isn't a receipt; use a real headline, letter or chart, or no image.
- **Replies:** 263 replies in a week averaged 20 views. Replies under 10k to 500k-follower finance accounts' fresh posts got
  180 to 386. Do ~15 a day there, early, each with a number or a question. Max one reply a day to the same small account.
  One-liners ("yes very true", "lovely", "come and join now") are banned.
- Pin the best action post so profile visitors have a reason to follow.
