"Sold" doesn't mean "paid." Here's the bit nobody explains 👇

When you sell a fund or ETF, two things happen:
1. The deal (you agree a price)
2. Settlement (cash and units actually swap hands)

Step 2 is the slow one. In the US and India, stock and ETF trades settle the next business day (T+1), and fund redemptions often take 1 to 3 business days. Weekends and holidays don't count.

On-chain, the deal and the settlement happen together, recorded on a public ledger, any day of the week.

The honest part: on-chain markets are younger and prices swing more. Learn first, start small.
