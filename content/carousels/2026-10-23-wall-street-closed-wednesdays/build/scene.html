New York · 1968

Wall Street closed every Wednesday.

for paperwork.
Not a holiday. From June to December 1968, the stock exchange shut one day a week so brokers could catch up.

The paper crunch

Every trade was a piece of paper.

carried by hand.
Share certificates were typed, signed and run across town. At the worst point, about $4 billion of shares were late reaching their new owners.

The fix

So we started cutting off days.

one at a time.
Settling a trade, the moment "sold" becomes "paid", took 5 business days. Then 3. Then 2.
