**ICP:** a 25 to 45 year old who saves or invests through funds, banks and payment apps, celebrating Diwali this weekend,
sending gifts and money. **Emotion:** unease ("wait, nobody's at the desk?"), then relief ("my money only moves when I move it").

the New York Fed was closed Sat-Sun, and 8 Feb was a Lunar New Year bank holiday in the Philippines (Wikipedia "Bangladesh Bank robbery"; Dawn, Fortune).
