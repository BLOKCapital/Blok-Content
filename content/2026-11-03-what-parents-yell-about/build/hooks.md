# Hook set: What parents yell about (Instagram Reel, 9:16, ~40s)

Brief (Sheetal, 3 Nov 2026): storytelling video. 2000-2010 parents scolded kids for playing outside too much; 2010-2020 for too much TV;
2022-2024 for games; 2025-2026 for Discord and AI agents. Close on money/AI (no "$10 vs thousands" line). Ends with a person saying
"Comment BANKER below" (lead magnet: 7 private-banker prompts for ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini that build a one-page Investment Policy Statement).
Audience (brand/instagram-strategy.md): 22-40 year olds who use or are curious about mutual funds/ETFs, grew up through these eras. No crypto words.

## Angles considered
A. Every scolded "addiction" became a career (athletes, YouTubers, streamers, AI builders) -> weak: needs stats we can't source, sounds like hustle content.
B. The thing nobody ever scolded you about: money. 25 years of yelling, money never made the list. Twist: the 2026 "addiction" (AI) is the first that can fix it. -> PICKED. Open loop held to second 24; ties straight into the BANKER prompts.
C. What parents will yell about in 2030 ("stop letting AI run your money") -> speculative, reads as a prediction.

## Hooks
H1 (picked) Comparison across eras + question/curiosity gap.
- On screen frame 0: "Every era, parents yell about something new." + rail of years 2000s . 2010s . 2022 . 2026 . ?
- Spoken: "Every few years, parents find a new thing to yell at kids about."
- Visual: linen background, a kid figure center; a coral speech bubble pops in from the left as the 2000s node lights.
- ICP: 28-year-old who was told to get inside, turn off the TV, put down the controller; feels nostalgic recognition, then a sting ("nobody taught me money").
H2 Conversational native: opens on the scold itself. Spoken: "Get in the house, it's getting dark!" then cut to rail.
H3 POV: "POV: you've been yelled at by your mom in three different decades."
