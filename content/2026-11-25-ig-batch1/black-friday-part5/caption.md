Here's the twist: the first Black Friday wasn't a sale. It was a crash. On Friday, 24 September 1869, two traders (one of them also named Jay, Jay Gould) had been trying to buy up so much gold that they could set the price. Then the US Treasury sold $4 million in gold, and the price of $100 in gold fell from about $163 to $133 (in paper dollars). Many traders were ruined. Gould had already started selling.

Different century, same lesson: the people with access move first.
